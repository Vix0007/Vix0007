### Research

**AEGIS: Adversarial Entropy-Guided Immune System** · [arXiv:2604.02149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02149) · 2026
cs.CR (primary), cs.LG (cross-list) · CC BY 4.0

A network intrusion detection approach that classifies traffic from flow-level timing and entropy statistics, without inspecting payloads. It uses a state-space sequence model with a Mamba-3 core. On the evaluation set in the paper it reports an F1 of 0.995 with sub-millisecond inference (about 262 µs); see the paper for the setup and limitations.

Dataset: [AEGIS-Adversarial-Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vix0007/AEGIS-Adversarial-Corpus) (908K sequences)

**HBEE: Human Behavioral Entropy Engine** · [arXiv:2605.07472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07472) · 2026

A pre-registered multi-agent LLM simulation study of insider-threat detection. Its main result is a detection inversion: an adaptive, LLM-driven insider drew less peer suspicion than a randomly chosen innocent agent, the opposite of the pre-registered prediction. The paper also states explicitly where the findings do not generalize.
